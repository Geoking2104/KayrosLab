## Sélection v2 (proposée à la validation du propriétaire)

### justice
- **Thèse (fr)** : la justice n'est pas l'intérêt du plus fort ; l'injustice, à la fin, ne rapporte pas plus que la justice
- **Thesis (en)** : justice is not the interest of the stronger; injustice, in the end, pays no more than justice
- Ancre : « The Republic » — "Then, my blessed Thrasymachus, injustice can never be more profitable than justice."

### verite
- **Thèse (fr)** : la vérité ne se décrète pas : qui aime la vérité en chaque chose est ami de la sagesse, non de l'opinion
- **Thesis (en)** : truth is not decreed: whoever loves truth in each thing is a friend of wisdom, not of opinion
- Ancre : « The Republic » — "But those who love the truth in each thing are to be called lovers of wisdom and not lovers of opinion."

### education
- **Thèse (fr)** : ce qui entre tôt dans une jeune âme y demeure : les premiers récits doivent être des modèles, car l'enfant les garde ineffaçables
- **Thesis (en)** : what enters a young soul early stays there: the first tales must be models, for the child keeps them indelibly
- Ancre : « The Republic » — "For a young person cannot judge what is allegorical and what is literal; anything that he receives into his mind at that age is likely to become indelible and unalterable; and therefore it is most important that the tales which the young first hear should be models of virtuous thoughts."

### pouvoir
- **Thèse (fr)** : le pouvoir n'est légitime qu'à contrecœur : la cité est le mieux gouvernée quand gouverner coûte
- **Thesis (en)** : power is legitimate only reluctantly: a city is best governed when governing costs
- Ancre : « The Republic » — "Whereas the truth is that the State in which the rulers are most reluctant to govern is always the best and most quietly governed, and the State in which they are most eager, the worst."

### vertu
- **Thèse (fr)** : la vertu ne s'achète pas : c'est elle qui donne tout le reste
- **Thesis (en)** : virtue is not bought: it is virtue that gives everything else
- Ancre : « Apology » — "I tell you that virtue is not given by money, but that from virtue comes money and every other good of man, public as well as private."

### amour
- **Thèse (fr)** : l'amour est le désir de son propre entier : nous cherchons l'autre comme la part perdue de nous-mêmes
- **Thesis (en)** : love is the desire of one's own whole: we seek the other as the lost part of ourselves
- Ancre : « Symposium » — "And the reason is that human nature was originally one and we were a whole, and the desire and pursuit of the whole is called love."
