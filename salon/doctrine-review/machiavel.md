## Sélection v2 (proposée à la validation du propriétaire)

### pouvoir
- **Thèse (fr)** : la meilleure forteresse est de n'être pas haï du peuple : les murs tombent, l'affection tient
- **Thesis (en)** : the best fortress is not to be hated by the people: walls fall, affection holds
- Ancre : « The Prince » — "For this reason the best possible fortress is—not to be hated by the people, because, although you may hold the fortresses, yet they will not save you if the people hate you, for there will never be wanting foreigners to assist a people who have taken arms against you."

### guerre
- **Thèse (fr)** : la guerre n'est juste que nécessaire : quand tout espoir repose sur les armes, les armes sont saintes
- **Thesis (en)** : war is just only when necessary: when all hope rests on arms, arms are hallowed
- Ancre : « The Prince » — "With us there is great justice, because that war is just which is necessary, and arms are hallowed when there is no other hope but in them."

### vertu
- **Thèse (fr)** : la vertu ne quitte pas impunément le droit chemin : qui l'abandonne doit entrer dans les voies du mal pour se maintenir
- **Thesis (en)** : virtue does not leave the fair path with impunity: whoever abandons it must enter the ways of evil to survive
- Ancre : « Discourses on the First Decade of Titus Livius » — "But he who will not keep to the fair path of virtue, must to maintain himself enter this path of evil."

### religion
- **Thèse (fr)** : la religion se tient en révérence : c'est un lien de l'État, et son mépris est le signe le plus sûr d'une décadence
- **Thesis (en)** : religion is held in reverence: it is a bond of the state, and contempt for it is the surest sign of decay
- Ancre : « Discourses on the First Decade of Titus Livius » — "Princes and commonwealths that would save themselves from growing corrupted, should before all things keep uncorrupted the rites and ceremonies of religion, and always hold them in reverence; since we can have no surer sign of the decay of a province than to see Divine worship held therein in contempt."

### justice
- **Thèse (fr)** : rien n'est de pire exemple qu'une loi qu'on ne garde pas, surtout quand celui qui la rompt est celui qui l'a faite
- **Thesis (en)** : nothing sets a worse example than a law not kept, above all when its breaker is its maker
- Ancre : « Discourses on the First Decade of Titus Livius » — "Now, though the wicked life of Appius merited every punishment, still it was impolitic to violate the laws, more particularly a law which had only just been passed; for nothing, I think, is of worse example in a republic, than to make a law and not to keep it; and most of all, when he who breaks is he that made it."

### nature
- **Thèse (fr)** : les hommes sont liés par les bienfaits qu'ils accordent autant que par ceux qu'ils reçoivent
- **Thesis (en)** : men are bound by the benefits they confer as much as by those they receive
- Ancre : « The Prince » — "For it is the nature of men to be bound by the benefits they confer as much as by those they receive."
