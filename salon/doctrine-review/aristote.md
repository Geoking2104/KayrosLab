## Sélection v2 (proposée à la validation du propriétaire)

### bonheur
- **Thèse (fr)** : le bonheur n'est pas un état mais une activité de l'âme ; on est heureux en acte, selon l'excellence
- **Thesis (en)** : happiness is not a state but an activity of the soul; one is happy in act, according to excellence
- Ancre : « The Nicomachean ethics of Aristotle » — "And by Human Excellence we mean not that of man’s body but that of his soul; for we call Happiness a working of the Soul."

### vertu
- **Thèse (fr)** : la vertu est un milieu entre deux excès ; dans l'ordre du bien, c'est le sommet, non une moyenne
- **Thesis (en)** : virtue is a mean between two extremes; in the order of the good it is the summit, not an average
- Ancre : « The Nicomachean ethics of Aristotle » — "And so, viewing it in respect of its essence and definition, Virtue is a mean state; but in reference to the chief good and to excellence it is the highest state possible."

### justice
- **Thèse (fr)** : la justice est la vertu des rapports : seule entre toutes, elle vise directement le bien d'autrui
- **Thesis (en)** : justice is the virtue of relations: alone among the virtues, it aims directly at another's good
- Ancre : « The Nicomachean ethics of Aristotle » — "And for this same reason Justice alone of all the Virtues is thought to be a good to others, because it has immediate relation to some other person, inasmuch as the Just man does what is advantageous to another, either to his ruler or fellow-subject."

### education
- **Thèse (fr)** : la morale ne s'apprend pas avant l'usage : d'abord de bonnes habitudes, ensuite des principes
- **Thesis (en)** : morals are not learned before practice: first good habits, then principles
- Ancre : « The Nicomachean ethics of Aristotle » — "Hence the necessity that he should have been well trained in habits, who is to study, with any tolerable chance of profit, the principles of nobleness and justice and moral philosophy generally."

### art
- **Thèse (fr)** : la poésie est plus philosophique que l'histoire : elle dit l'universel, l'histoire le particulier
- **Thesis (en)** : poetry is more philosophical than history: it speaks the universal, history the particular
- Ancre : « The Poetics of Aristotle » — "Poetry, therefore, is a more philosophical and a higher thing than history: for poetry tends to express the universal, history the particular."

### pouvoir
- **Thèse (fr)** : la cité n'est pas née pour vivre seulement, mais pour vivre bien : le pouvoir se juge à la fin qu'il sert
- **Thesis (en)** : the city was founded not for life alone but for the good life: power is judged by the end it serves
- Ancre : « Politics: A Treatise on Government » — "And when many villages so entirely join themselves together as in every respect to form but one society, that society is a city, and contains in itself, if I may so speak, the end and perfection of government: first founded that we might live, but continued that we may live happily."
