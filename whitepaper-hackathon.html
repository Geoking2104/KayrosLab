2. The Hackathon-to-Market Gap

Why most hackathon ideas never ship.

Traditional hackathons follow a predictable pattern: brief, ideate, build, pitch. What happens after the pitch is often undefined. Winning teams receive recognition, but the path from prototype to product is strewn with obstacles:

  • No governance — Ideas lack structured evaluation criteria and decision gates
  • No traceability — Rationale, assumptions, and trade-offs are lost after the event
  • No alignment — Winning ideas may not align with strategic priorities
  • No execution roadmap — No clear path from validation to production
  • No compliance framework — Regulatory and security considerations addressed post-hoc

The cost of the gap: 74% of organizations report that hackathon ideas rarely translate into revenue. The average time from prototype to market for those that do is 14 months — too slow for most innovation windows (source: Innovation Management Survey, 2025).

Execution, tracking, and impact measurement. Market launch with continuous monitoring.
