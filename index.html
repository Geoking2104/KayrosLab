No black box

Confident opinions are easy. Governed decisions are not.

Meetings compress uncertainty into opinions, the strongest voice wins, and the numbers get retrofitted afterwards. KayrosLab keeps the whole chain inspectable — claims, sources, numbers and objections — until reality runs the experiment.

GO

Proceed

The committee's evidence and conditions are satisfied. The accountable human accepts and moves to execution.

CONDITIONAL_GO

Proceed under conditions

Friction remains: a missing proof, an unresolved objection. The decision passes only with attached conditions.

NO_GO

Do not proceed

A veto holder blocks it. The dossier records the objection and the rationale — for good, not forever.

Vote instructs · veto decides. The verdict never overrides governance: it stays consultative until a human accepts, attaches conditions, or overrides a veto.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is KayrosLab?
A governed decision workshop. You state one real decision; a committee of role-bound agents with opposing duties investigates the evidence, attacks its own assumptions, and returns a verdict — GO, conditional GO or NO-GO. The verdict stays consultative until the accountable human accepts it, attaches conditions, or overrides a veto, and the whole dossier stays inspectable.
Is it a trained model?
No. KayrosLab is a governed LLM stack, not a crystal ball. It is an orchestrator that drives real models — Ollama quant-aware on your machine, or Mistral / Claude through the backend — behind layered memory, deterministic Monte-Carlo numbers and human gates with veto rights.
Can it run on our own machines?
Yes. Tier P1 is local-sovereign: quant-aware Ollama inference on your infrastructure, so no data leaves your perimeter. Tier P2 is a governed cloud with optional Postgres for multi-instance persistence. You choose per tenant.
Which channels does it work in?
Slack, Microsoft Teams and Discord each become a decision room — with signatures and idempotence, Adaptive Cards or Block Kit, one-click connection and encrypted secrets. The console remains the operational home, and a Developer Portal MCP server exposes scoped tools for agentic API consumers.
How do you handle privacy and consent?
Official connectors or authorized exports only — no LinkedIn scraping. Hybrid and impersonator agents require explicit consent and provenance, never fabricate private facts, and always label simulated feedback as a simulation. It cannot be presented as a real quote, endorsement or behavioral prediction.
Does a verdict ever override a human?
Never. Vote instructs, veto decides. Sensitive syntheses open a human gate; the human accepts, passes under conditions, or overrides a veto with justification — and every action is written to an append-only audit trail.
Is it open source?
The repository is public and the engine core is zero-dependency for inspectability, but KayrosLab is distributed under a proprietary licence. You can read the architecture, run the core tests, and deploy the engine; commercial use follows the licence terms. See the repository for details.
