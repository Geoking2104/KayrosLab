One rehearsal. Two high-stakes moments.

Use the same governed process for an internal executive decision or a customer buying committee. The agents change roles; evidence, traceability and human judgment stay constant.

Executive decision rehearsal

Test a strategic option before the real committee meets.

  • Combine system agents, custom experts and authorized executive profiles.
  • Expose objections, dependencies and decision criteria.
  • Leave the final decision with the accountable human.

Sales Oracle / RFP rehearsal

Pressure-test the offer before it reaches the real buyer.

  • Map finance, security, legal, operations and sponsor concerns.
  • Trace every objection to evidence, a role rule or a labelled assumption.
  • Strengthen the proposal and negotiation position before submission.

See the decision, not a catalogue of features.

The dossier turns a multi-agent run into four things an executive can use in the next meeting.

  1. Stakeholder mapWho influences, approves or can veto the decision.
  2. Objection heatmapWhich concerns are decisive and which are secondary.
  3. Evidence checklistWhat proof is missing and where each claim comes from.
  4. Human gateWho approves, rejects or requests revision—with a retained audit trail.

Simulation with clear boundaries.

KayrosLab rehearses a decision; it does not claim to read minds or predict what a real stakeholder will say.

Inspect the architecture Read the governance brief
  • Only consented and authorized profiles are used.
  • Simulated statements are labelled as simulation.
  • Human approval gates remain binding.
  • Sources, assumptions and actions remain auditable.
  • Sovereign or cloud deployment is available.
