THE KAYROSLAB MODEL

A structured 8-step process.
Hybrid AI + Human governance.

Unlike free-form LLM conversations, KayrosLab follows a cyclical workflow inspired by best practices in strategy and foresight, industrialized by specialized AI agents and formalized Human-in-the-Loop. The 4th step Position confronts the idea with the market through automated competitive analysis, the 7th Project transforms the decision into a piloted trajectory, the 8th Realize ensures execution and measurement, then the cycle loops back to Listen.

01

Listen

Noise reduction, qualification and scoring of weak signals.

→ Qualified Signals
02

Map

Trend network, bisociative bridges and strategic relationships.

→ Network + Bridges
03

Build

Scenarios, Collision Mode and structured collaborative briefs.

→ Scenarios + Brief
04

Position

Ontological competitive analysis: Cytoscape.js graph, property inspector, query playground, OWL export.

→ Ontology + Gap Analysis
05

Test

Multi-agent future-proofing: Critic, Devil's Advocate, Red Team.

→ Challenged Ideas
06

Arbitrate

Multi-criteria votes, Working Groups and structured final decision.

→ Decision + Deliverable
07

Project

Trajectory, roadmap, probabilistic simulation and learning loop.

→ Roadmap + KPIs
08

Realize

Pilot, deployment, review: tracked milestones and actual impact vs projected.

→ Actual vs Projected → loop

The differentiation gap directly feeds the Kayros Index and prepares the Red Team.

Model a strategic decision with the perspectives of the executives who will challenge it. The result is a decision dossier, not a synthetic endorsement.

  3. 3Run the governed voteCapture GO, conditional GO, NO-GO, vetoes, objections and the evidence requested by each role.
  4. 4Arbitrate as a humanCompare positions, expose friction and let the accountable executive make the final decision.

TECHNOLOGY ARCHITECTURE

A zero-dependency engine.
A governed architecture.

KayrosLab is built around a pure Node.js core (ESM, zero-dependency) that orchestrates AI agents, applies Human-in-the-Loop governance, and computes trajectories deterministically — without ever inventing numbers.

Zero-Dependency Core

Pure Node 20+ ESM engine — zero npm dependencies. 81+ unit tests. Usable client-side (browser imports) and server-side (Fastify).

Multi-Agent Orchestration

Plan-and-Solve + ReAct. 6 specialized agents: Planner, Critic, Devil's Advocate, Red Team, Bisociator, Synthesizer — each with a defined role in the ideation pipeline.

Human-in-the-Loop Governance

4 gate types (expert_review, red_team_veto, comex_arbitrage, output_censor) and 4 human roles. Absolute veto with mandatory justification. Gates persisted to disk.

Deterministic Calculations

Monte-Carlo (seeded PRNG), Kayros Index scoring (5 strategic × 6 technical dimensions), ROI, TCO, NPV — all computed, never hallucinated. The LLM provides hypotheses; the engine calculates.

Pluggable LLM Providers

Mock (offline), Ollama (sovereign), Anthropic Claude (proxy), HTTP Backend. RoutingPolicy with automatic failover and Circuit Breaker per provider (3 strikes, 30s cooldown). Client never sees API keys.

Vector Memory

InMemory or Qdrant. Semantic recall via cosine similarity with ideaId filtering. Used for agent context (ReAct loop) and the Monitor/Listen feedback loop.

Resilience & Circuit Breaker

Exponential backoff with full jitter. 3-state Circuit Breaker (CLOSED/OPEN/HALF_OPEN). withResilience() composes retry + breaker + fallback. Everything is non-blocking.

Ontological Positioning

14 entity types, typed properties, oriented relationships. Web + GitHub scraping. OWL RDF/XML export. Cytoscape.js graph with inspector, query playground, competitive gap analysis.

Conversational Connectors

Slack (Block Kit, modals, slash commands), Teams/Discord (Adaptive Cards). Abstract View/Action translated to native formats. Account linking via single-use cryptographic tokens.

Strategic Steering & KPIs

Kanban with gates, per-stage configurable scorecards, weighted votes, impact tracking (projected vs actual), leaderboards, campaigns with moderation, CSV export, funnel analysis.

COMPLIANCE &amp; SOVEREIGNTY

An architecture designed for the most
demanding compliance environments.

KayrosLab was designed from the ground up to meet the sovereignty, traceability and security requirements of regulated organizations and strategic sectors.

Data Sovereignty

  • Sovereign local deployment (Ollama + Qdrant) with no data transfer outside
  • Zero dependency on US hyperscalers for strategic data processing
  • Data exploitation and valorization exclusively by your organization

Certifications &amp; Alignments

  • Local architecture designed to facilitate HDS compliance (Health Data Hosting)
  • Alignment with the European Health Data Space (EHDS)
  • Compatible with French and European digital sovereignty requirements

Regulatory Compliance

  • Native GDPR compliance (data minimization, right to erasure, consent)
  • Aligned with NIS2 and DORA requirements (resilience, traceability, risk governance)
  • Relevant ISO standards: ISO 27001 (information security), ISO 27701 (privacy management) and ISO 42001 (AI management systems)
  • Preparation for SOC 2 Type II certification (security, availability, confidentiality and data integrity)
  • Complete traceability of decisions and processing for internal and external audits

Auditability &amp; Governance

  • Complete timestamped timeline of all steps and decisions
  • Traceable and exportable Kayros Index (KI) scoring
  • AI agent logs + human decisions for audits and risk committees
  • Structured human gates (COMEX veto, expert validation) with traceability
