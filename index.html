Clear outcomes

Confident opinions are easy. Governed decisions are not.

Meetings compress uncertainty into opinions; the strongest voice wins. KayrosLab keeps claims, sources, numbers and objections inspectable until you commit.

GO

Proceed

Evidence and conditions are satisfied. The accountable human accepts and moves to execution.

CONDITIONAL_GO

Proceed under conditions

Friction remains: a missing proof or unresolved objection. The decision passes only with attached conditions.

NO_GO

Do not proceed

A veto holder blocks it. The dossier records the objection and the rationale — reopenable when evidence changes.

Vote instructs · veto decides. The verdict never overrides governance: it stays consultative until a human accepts, attaches conditions, or overrides a veto.

FAQ

Questions leaders ask first

What exactly is KayrosLab?
A governed decision workshop. You state one real decision; a committee of role-bound agents with opposing duties investigates the evidence, challenges assumptions, and returns a verdict — GO, conditional GO or NO-GO. The verdict stays consultative until the accountable human accepts it, attaches conditions, or overrides a veto.
Will it replace our executives?
No. It is an executive committee you can summon for rehearsal and challenge — not a crystal ball and not an autopilot. Humans arbitrate; vetoes remain real.
Can it run on our own machines?
Yes. You can keep inference and data on your infrastructure when sovereignty matters, or use a governed cloud. You choose per workspace.
Where do teams run missions?
In the console, and optionally in Slack, Microsoft Teams or Discord as decision rooms. The dossier remains the source of truth.
What are personified agents?
Two complementary paths. Role packs (CFO, CTO, CHRO / HR, Legal, Sales…) encode the typical characteristics and veto reflexes of a function. Real-profile import — optionally via CrystalKnows when you have an authorized, consented personality profile — lets you stress-test a decision or mission against how that stakeholder tends to communicate and object. Feedback is always labelled Simulation; it is never a real quote, endorsement or behavioural prediction.
How do you handle privacy and consent?
Official connectors or authorized exports only — no scraping. CrystalKnows and other profile imports require explicit consent and provenance. Simulated stakeholder feedback is always labelled as simulation — never as a real quote or endorsement.
Does a verdict ever override a human?
Never. Vote instructs, veto decides. Sensitive syntheses open a human gate; every action is written to an append-only audit trail.
