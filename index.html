01 · WHAT IT CHANGES, CONCRETELY

Three decisions KayrosLab helps you make.

One principle: your question is investigated by agents with opposing duties — and nothing is decided without you.

Launch — or not

“Should we launch this now, and under what conditions?”

You are weighing a launch. In the console, you hand the question to your committee. Each agent examines it through its own duty: economics, feasibility, compliance. The collective returns an argued verdict — GO, conditional GO or NO-GO — with objections and the conditions to meet. You arbitrate: accept, attach conditions, or request another pass.

  • An argued verdict, not one more opinion
  • Objections and conditions consolidated in a single dossier
  • The final call stays human, with an audit trail
Decide where the team already works

“Our decisive conversations are scattered across Slack and Teams.”

Bind a Slack, Microsoft Teams or Discord channel to a collective. On a simple mention, it investigates the question raised in the channel — and every decision opens a durable thread with analyses, replies and arbitration kept. Decisions stop getting lost between two conversations.

  • Every channel becomes a governed decision room
  • One durable thread per decision, resumable anytime
  • Relaunch the same collective with fresh information
The right committee for each decision

“I’m missing the risk view — and a skeptical customer.”

Compose your committee: ready-to-use business agents, custom experts, or hybrid profiles that draw — with explicit consent — on how your stakeholders communicate and decide. Some agents can hold veto power. Every role, rule and boundary is inspectable.

  • Instant business agents, configurable experts
  • Consented profiles, never scraped data
  • Explicit vetoes, like a real committee
02 · HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. No decision without you.

KayrosLab separates investigation, disagreement and arbitration: an attractive conclusion never becomes an automatic decision.

  1. Connect your channels

    Web console, Slack, Teams or Discord: bind your workspaces and compose your collectives in minutes.

  2. Ask the question

    The collective investigates: individual analyses, objections, conditions, an argued verdict — all kept in a durable dossier.

  3. Arbitrate on evidence

    Accept the consensus, pass it under conditions, or request a re-evaluation. The final decision — and its justification — belong to you.

03 · PROJECT BEFORE YOU COMMIT

A number to inform you — not to decide for you.

When your indicators allow it, KayrosLab extends the observed trajectory with a point forecast and an uncertainty range — stored as a simulation, never as a verdict.

  • Built on at least 20 real observations
  • Stored per tenant, labelled as simulation
  • Human review mandatory under high uncertainty
