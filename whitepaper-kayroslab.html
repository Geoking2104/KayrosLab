KayrosLab — Complete White Paper

The governed ideation methodology: from weak signal to strategic decision.

1. The Strategic Ideation Challenge

Organizations today face a paradox: more data than ever, yet less certainty about the future. Traditional strategic planning methods are too slow, too rigid, and too dependent on linear thinking. AI-powered chatbots can generate ideas but lack structure, governance, and accountability. KayrosLab bridges this gap by providing a governed, agentic ideation system that combines the creative power of AI with the rigor of structured decision-making.

2. The KayrosLab Process

The KayrosLab process is organized around 8 cyclical steps, each with specific agents, tools, and governance gates:

  1. Listen — Weak signal detection and qualification
  2. Map — Trend network construction and bisociation
  3. Build — Scenario generation and collaborative briefs
  4. Position — Ontological competitive analysis
  5. Test — Multi-agent challenge (Critic, Devil's Advocate, Red Team)
  6. Arbitrate — Multi-criteria voting and human decision
  7. Project — Probabilistic trajectory, roadmap, KPIs
  8. Realize — Execution, tracking, and impact measurement

The loop then cycles back to Listen, creating a continuous improvement engine.

3. Technology Architecture

KayrosLab's technology core is built on three pillars:

  • Plan-and-Solve + ReAct Orchestration — Dynamic planning, iterative reasoning with tools, shared and vector memory
  • Multi-Agent System — Specialized agents (Planner, Critic, Devil's Advocate, Red Team, Bisociator, Synthesizer)
  • Human Governance Layer — Structured gates, multi-criteria voting, COMEX veto, expert validation

4. Compliance and Sovereignty

KayrosLab is designed for regulated environments. Key compliance features include:

  • Sovereign local deployment (Ollama + Qdrant) with zero data egress
  • Full auditability with timestamped timelines and agent logs
  • GDPR, NIS2, DORA alignment
  • HDS-compatible architecture
  • SOC 2 Type II preparation

5. Deployment Tiers

KayrosLab offers three deployment tiers to match different organizational needs: P0 Standalone (free, single HTML file), P1 Sovereign (local deployment on your infrastructure), and P2 Cloud (cloud deployment with maximized AI capabilities). All tiers share the same core engine, ensuring consistency and upgradeability.

6. Conclusion

KayrosLab represents a new paradigm in strategic ideation: a governed, agentic system that transforms weak signals into auditable strategic decisions. By combining AI automation with structured human governance, it enables organizations to innovate faster, decide better, and execute with confidence.
